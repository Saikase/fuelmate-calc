# Privacy Policy & Terms of Service for FuelMate

**Last Updated:** November 12, 2026  
**Application:** FuelMate - Fuel Cost Calculator (`com.fuelmate.fuelcalc`)

## 1. Privacy Policy

### Zero Personal Data Collection
FuelMate is a standalone utility application designed to calculate fuel consumption and travel expenses. We respect your personal privacy:
- We **do not** collect, store, or transmit your name, phone number, email address, contact list, or real-time GPS location coordinates.
- All vehicle preferences, custom vehicle profiles, and saved trip histories are stored strictly locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage. You can delete this data at any time inside the app.

### Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)
FuelMate displays banner advertisements powered by **Google AdMob**.
- Google AdMob may use anonymized mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID) to serve personalized or contextual advertisements.
- Data collected by Google AdMob is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google AdMob Policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).
- Users can opt out of personalized ads or reset their Advertising ID anytime through Android Device Settings: **Settings > Google > Ads**.

### Permissions
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required solely to retrieve current benchmark fuel prices from public price feeds and display Google AdMob advertisements.

## 2. Terms of Service

### Estimation & Disclaimer
FuelMate provides calculations based on official manufacturer-rated fuel economy specifications and public retail fuel price references. Actual fuel expenditures may vary due to road conditions, traffic congestion, cargo load, air conditioning usage, driving habits, and individual gas station pricing. Calculations are for personal estimation and planning purposes only.
